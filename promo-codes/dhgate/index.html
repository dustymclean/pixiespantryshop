Why isn’t my DHgate coupon working?

If a code is rejected, it is almost always one of four things: the offer has ended since it was last verified, your cart is under the minimum spend, the items are excluded (sale items and new releases usually are), or another discount is already applied and the store only allows one. Try it on a clean cart first. Codes are re-checked against the network feeds every week and anything dead is moved down to the expired section rather than left sitting at the top of the page.
